TV Fitness Celebrity Forbes Riley Loses 703 Pounds

From the original Forbes Factor · August 12, 2017

TV Celebrity Host and America’s Most Loved “Health and Fitness Innovator” Forbes Riley, launched the first ever “Forbes Riley 10-Week SpinGym SlimDown Challenge,” which resulted in 30 participants losing more than 700 lbs.

Having had tremendous success with her first ever SpinGym SlimDown WeightLoss Challenge, Round 2 is being held, LIVE in St. Petersburg, Florida.

learn on how to get fit and healthy, I am now using to help people transform their lives”  Forbes Riley ST. PETERSBURG, FL (PRWEB) FEBRUARY 06, 2013

In the world of fad diets and get-slim-quick scams, celebrity TV host, National Fitness Hall of Fame inductee and creator of the SpinGym fitness sensation, Forbes Riley, stands alongside her fitness mentor gurus Jack Lalanne, Tony Horton of P90x and Jillian Michaels of NBC’s Biggest Loser, as the real deal. Someone who gets real results with real people.

Forbes Riley, who has made a career inspiring people across the globe to adopt healthier lifestyles that improve their lives forever, has amplified her mission and opened her film studio, The Forbes Riley Studios, to work one-on-one with people. She is launching the book “e.a.t – a Journal for What You Eat, and for What’s Eating You” and developed a virtual community called http://www.FitwithForbes.com to expand her reach and impact.

This past fall, Riley launched the first ever “Forbes Riley 10-Week SpinGym SlimDown Challenge,” which resulted in nearly 30 participants shedding 703 lbs. This is much more than a fitness and weight loss program—its focus is on empowering people with information and tools to make life-long changes to live life with greater focus and abundance.

This is a unique fitness program designed by Forbes Riley to address life holistically: health, wealth and happiness and how your whole life “FITS” together. Round two of this Challenge has just launched, but those interested are still able to participate remotely through access to streaming video and other web-based technologies at: http://www.forbesmembersite.com/LIVE

“I have spent my life overweight, and the information that took me a lifetime to learn on how to get fit and healthy, I am now using to help people transform their lives in just 10 weeks. My product, the SpinGym, is the key to this success. This powerful fitness device that can facilitate a whole-body workout, is so small and portable that you can take your “gym” with you everywhere,” said Riley.

One Challenge participant, Sashanna Roman, having completed the 10-week program, described her experience with Riley and the Challenge: “I think of Forbes Riley as ‘The Truth.’ What I mean is that she has shown me that the truth about gaining weight and holding onto weight has very little to do with food and a lot to do with how we let our stories, and how we think and feel about our stories, drive our behaviors. It’s more than just decreasing numbers on a scale—it’s a way of living.”

Ms. Roman, a research associate for a cancer research institute and single mother of two young children, has decided to commit to Riley’s second SpinGym SlimDown Challenge, saying, “Forbes is amazing and the changes I’ve experienced are just the beginning. Forbes has not only changed my life, but the good habits I’m developing are being passed on to my kids, changing their lives, making them healthier for a lifetime. I can never thank her enough for all she has done.”

“I’ve worked on many different aspects of my life before from health and wellness to education, business and psychology, but this is the first time I have ever worked on all of these themes together. It’s because Forbes doesn’t only address one of these issues but she shows people how to connect the dots—that one issue affects the other—that for the first time, change is not only happening but lasting,” said Robin King, successful St. Petersburg Restauranteur.

About SpinGym™
Portable, compact, and weighing less than one pound, SpinGym™ is a revolutionary new fitness product. Its award-winning, modern German design combines the physics of inertia and isokinetics to create a completely unique way to train, strengthen and sculpt the muscles of the arms, shoulders, back and core in just 5 minutes a day. This principle is called GRT, Gyrotronic Resistance Training™, and unlike free weights or gym machines, each rotation of the SpinGym™ energizes and activates all the muscles of the upper body simultaneously–a breakthrough in the world of fitness. SpinGym™ not only provides resistance, but because of the rotational forces created–it becomes a complete fat melting, calorie burning total body gym.
