# Top Model Cara

Date: 2019-03-04
Original URL: https://forbesfactor.com/whats-new-3/top-model-cara/
Categories: Forbes &amp; Friends, WHAT'S NEW?

---

Cara Delevingne (https://www.wmagazine.com/topic/cara-delevingne) was always destined to be more than a model. And since she was launched in 2011 by Christopher Bailey at Burberry, Delevingne’s personality and talent has been rather irrepressible. She’s covered countless magazines, become an outspoken voice (https://www.wmagazine.com/story/cara-delevingne-sexual-fluidity) for the LGBTQ+ community, and an expert troll (https://www.wmagazine.com/story/cara-delevingne-ashley-benson-relationship-trolling) of the paparazzi and internet who like to make a little too much of any woman she hangs out with. Not to mention, she has made one of the more successful model-to-movie star transitions in recent memory, from her magnetic performance in Paper Towns to blockbuster fame in Valerian and the City of a Thousand Planets and Suicide Squad. But let’s not forget that she was—still is—a unique and remarkable model, as we were all reminded by her recent, triumphant return to the runway (https://www.wmagazine.com/story/cara-delevingne-burberry-runway) for Bailey’s final Burberry show this year. And she has always been one of W‘s favorite models. And we’ve gone everywhere together—on the streets of London, to the shores of Sicily, channeling nouveau grunge for Mert and Marcus. Here, find a look inside the pages of W at the many times she’s been photographed for the magazine.
