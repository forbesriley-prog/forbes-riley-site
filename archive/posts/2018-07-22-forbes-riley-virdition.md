Morgan James’s new release “Virdition: The Secret to Fast Tracking YOUR Path to Stardom” (https://amzn.to/2PIOHv5) by Forbes Riley and Will Quinones is Hollywood’s new handbook for success. As Uber impacted taxi services and AirBnB shook-up the hotel industry, Virdition’s is the long awaited disruptive solution to the antiquated casting rules of Hollywood. In the digital age, talented individuals in any genre—from stuntmen and musicians to actresses, singers, dancers, and even make-up artists—can create their brand, steer their career, and master the art of “virtual auditioning” with Virdition’s guidance, tips, and insider secrets.

Social media outlets such as YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and SnapChat provide performers with opportunities to showcase their talent to wide audiences immediately online—a far cry from the days of paper headshots and “cattle call” auditions. Virdition (https://amzn.to/2PIOHv5) helps readers leverage digital tools to achieve success, and the Virdition.com platform will open the marketplace for stardom, placing it within anyone’s grasp.

With the practical tools in Virdition, anyone with talent and a dream can carve out their place in the billion-dollar entertainment industry. However, this book also is a dynamic read for entrepreneurs, sales teams and public speakers who want to learn how to leverage their personal brand and rise above their competition.

Endorsed by veteran performers like triple threat/co-host of “American Idol” Paula Abdul, actor/soap opera star Kevin Spiritas, and even “Shark Tank’s” Kevin Harrington, Virdition serves as a door-opening opportunity and a must read.

“Virdition is making it easier for those in the entertainment industry to connect with agents/casting directors. This book is inspiring, practical, and concise,” says theatrical agent Dora Whitaker.

With more than 20 years of experience in the entertainment industry, Forbes Riley shares her insights and valuable tips for overcoming obstacles on the path to success, based on her own journey pursuing a career as an actress, dancer, TV host, and ultimately using her skills to launch a multi-million empire, and open her own state-of-the-art television and production studio in St Petersburg, Florida.

Timothy Hodgkiss remarks, “Forbes’s positive message and uplifting tone really make you feel that you can achieve anything you want in life and business, if you are brave enough to fight for them. The book shows the importance of branding and social media and how they can be leveraged to create opportunities.”

Riley and Virdition CEO Will Quinones partnered to write the book in order to help others achieve their entertainment career dreams, while providing the Virdition online virtual audition platform so that artists can display and broadcast their talents to industry insiders and audiences digitally. Both Riley and Quinones are passionate about helping struggling artists of all levels achieve their dreams.
