Questions and travel

Before You Say Yes.

Can I attend if I have never spoken professionally?

Yes. FRedX is designed for both new and experienced speakers. Whether it is your first time on stage or your hundredth, Forbes and her team guide you step by step to refine your message, boost your confidence and deliver a professional, powerful presentation.

Will I really get to speak on stage?

Yes. Every in-person attendee gets dedicated five-minute stage time that is professionally filmed, photographed and coached, so you walk away with a powerful video asset for your brand.

Do I need to prepare my five-minute talk in advance?

Bring a draft if you have one. Thursday is built around message development and preparation, and your talk is sharpened with Forbes and the team before you film it on Friday.

Is the speaking video a complete talk or a highlight reel?

You receive a professionally edited video of your complete five-minute talk, delivered after the event. Whether a separate short highlight cut is included is being confirmed with the team.

When will I receive my edited video and photographs?

Both are delivered after the event. The exact delivery timeline and the number of edited photographs are being confirmed with the team; ask at support@teamforbesriley.com before you enrol if it matters to your plans.

Are airfare and accommodations included?

Travel and hotel are not part of the ticket. The ticket covers all three days at the studio, the production and the catered lunches. Fly into Tampa International, arrive Wednesday, January 27, and leave Sunday, January 31.

Payment plans, cancellations and transfers

Payment plan availability and the cancellation or transfer policy are confirmed by the team when you enrol. Email support@teamforbesriley.com and you will get a straight answer before any money changes hands.

Will my talk be published on YouTube?

Many FRedX talks are published on the FRedX YouTube channel and the Forbes Riley playlist. Whether yours is published, and how you may use your footage commercially, is confirmed with you by the team.

I still have questions. How do I reach the team?

Email support@teamforbesriley.com and a real person will answer.
