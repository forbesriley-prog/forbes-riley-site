# Movie Producer Brian Grazer loves SpinGym

I was lucky enough to run into Brian backstage in NYC at Fox & Friends.  He asked about SpinGym, tried it, loved it and even posted it to Twitter!

[IMAGE: https://forbesfactor.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/Brian-Grazer-Twitter-Screen-shot-2015-04-10-at-11.23.15-AM.png]

He co-founded Imagine Entertainment (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imagine_Entertainment) in 1986, with Ron Howard (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Howard). The films they produced have grossed over $13 billion.The movies include four for which Grazer was personally nominated for an Academy Award (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award): Splash (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Splash_(film)) (1984), Apollo 13 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_13_(film)) (1995), A Beautiful Mind (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Beautiful_Mind_(film)) (2001), and Frost/Nixon (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frost/Nixon_(film)) (2008). His films and TV series have been nominated for 43 Academy Awards, and 131 Emmys (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy).

In 2002, Grazer won an Oscar for Best Picture (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Picture) for A Beautiful Mind (shared with Ron Howard). In 2007, he was named one of Time (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_(magazine))‘s “100 Most Influential People in the World (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_100)“.

Grazer began his career as a producer developing television projects. While executive-producing TV pilots at Paramount Pictures (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paramount_Pictures) in the early 1980s, he met current long-time friend and business partner Ron Howard (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Howard).

He produced his first feature-film, Night Shift (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Night_Shift_(film)), in 1982, directed by Howard. Grazer and Howard teamed up again for Splash (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Splash_(film)) in 1984, which Grazer produced and co-wrote. Splash earned him an Oscar (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award) nomination for Best Original Screenplay (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Original_Screenplay) of 1984.

In 1986, Grazer and Howard co-founded Imagine Entertainment (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imagine_Entertainment), which became one of Hollywood’s most prolific and successful production companies. Over the years, Grazer’s films and TV shows have been nominated for a total of 43 Academy Awards (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Awards), and 131 Emmys (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy). At the same time, his movies have generated more than $13.5 billion in worldwide theatrical, music, and video grosses.

Grazer’s early film successes include Parenthood (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parenthood_(1989_film)) (1989) and Backdraft (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backdraft_(film)) (1991) and Apollo 13 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_13_(film)) (1995), In 1998, he earned two major honors: he was given his own star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollywood_Walk_of_Fame), 

In 2001, Grazer won an Academy Award for Best Picture for A Beautiful Mind (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Beautiful_Mind_(film)).

In 2015 Grazer published his book A Curious Mind: The Secret to a Bigger Life, in which he discusses conversations with interesting people, many of whom inspired his work.
