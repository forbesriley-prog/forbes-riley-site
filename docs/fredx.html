Questions?

Asked before every FRedX.

Will I really get to speak on stage?

Yes. Every in-person attendee gets dedicated five-minute stage time that is professionally filmed, photographed and coached, so you walk away with a powerful video asset for your brand.

Do I need prior speaking experience to attend?

Not at all. FRedX is designed for both new and experienced speakers. Whether it is your first time on stage or your hundredth, Forbes and her team guide you step by step to refine your message, boost your confidence and deliver a professional, powerful presentation.

What should I bring?

Your five-minute talk in draft, two outfits you feel great in (one for stage, one for the photo shoot) and an open mind. Everything else, lunch included, is handled.

Email support@teamforbesriley.com and a real person will answer.
