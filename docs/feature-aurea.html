MEET LEGACY MAKER – AUREA MCGARRY

Aurea McGarry is CEO and Founder of Legacy Maker® International, an Emmy Award winning TV show host and producer, Author, Speaker, Direct Sales Master Coach, and President of Live Your Legacy Summit. Aurea McGarry who is better known as the Legacy Maker was personally trained by some of the best in the world. Mary Kay Ash, Zig Ziglar, John Maxwell, and Mark Victor Hanson to name a few.

Aurea McGarry’s tools and techniques for people, sales, and media are some of the most sought after in the country. Aurea, as a Legacy Maker®, incorporates strategic actions for the individual, corporations, and product exposure for optimal success. Aurea volunteers her time to many charities. As a professional Auctioneer she has served the Boys & Girls Club of America, Nancy Lieberman Foundation, Toys for Tots to name a few.

Aurea has served for the last five years as a judge and member of the southeast EMMY awards. She is a member of The National Association of Professional Women, eWomen Network, WOAMTEC and several other groups which support women’s empowerment. She actively promotes Change for Georgia supporting our troops, Noah’s Ark Animal and Children Rehabilitation Center among many others. Her passion is supporting women, children, animals, and our soldiers.

Aurea is also winner of the “Passion to Profits” Award from Tory Johnson’s Spark & Hustle national event series  Aurea causes individuals and organizations to go above and beyond. Her people skills and programs turn dreams into goals with strategies.

Now a Proud Cancer Sur-Thriver since 2000, she has authored several books such as“I Won’t Survive … I’ll Thrive!” She lives near Atlanta happily married to her Prince Charming, Brian McGarry, is a mother of two beautiful daughters, Angelica and Alyssa and mother in law to Dr. Matthew Boudreau.

Favorite Quote: “If your ship doesn’t come in…take a taxi! ~ Aurea McGarry ~
