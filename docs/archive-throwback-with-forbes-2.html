1996 My amazing dogs - PEEVE & SAFI

Peeve was my first dog as an adult - and he was beyond smart and fun! Part dalmatian part pitbull, we got him as a rescue. Safi, a cocker spaniel was a gift from a dear friend and her name is Swahili for pure - I had just returned from Africa and it just fit -- Oh, and its was always fun to say Peeve's name -- he was MY PET PEEVE!

2002 - The best kids ever!

Okay - every mom probably says that - and it IS true. On dec 4th I was blessed with healthy amazing twins - Makenna and Ryker. Makenna is named for the romantic movie Somewhere in Time, starring Christopher - I worked opposite him in a Broadway show and always dreamed I'd have a daughter named Makenna - Ryker is homage to my passion for Star Trek - and Commander Riker - played by Jonathon Frakes was my inspiration!

1991 - Heading West to Pursue Dreams of Hollywood

I was born in Brooklyn and grew up on Long Island - complete with the thick accent! I headed into THE CITY as they call it and worked for 10 years on my dream of acting from Broadway and Off-Broadway shows to Soap Operas (As The World Turns, Another World) - which were good to me - then I headed to pursue my dream in Los Angeles!
