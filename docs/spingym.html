How it works

Gyrotronic resistance training

The secret is the vibrating, slightly irregular load created by the rotation of SpinGym’s central weighted disc. That is what creates resistance, and it is why all of your upper body and core muscles are engaged at once. With up to 20 pounds of resistance on each pull, you double your heart rate in under a minute.

It tones arms, shoulders, back and abs deeper than bands or dumbbells, and the DVD routines are designed to sculpt, not bulk. Forbes conditioned her quads and hamstrings with it after ACL replacement surgery.

  1. Pull.

    Hold the handles, pull apart, and let the disc spin up.

  2. Hold the rhythm.

    Keep the disc spinning. The irregular load does the work.

  3. Five minutes, three times a day.

    The SpinGym Promise: commit to that and you will quickly feel and see changes.
