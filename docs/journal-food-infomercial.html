My Favorite Food Infomercial

By Forbes Riley · July 5, 2016

I’ve hosted a lot of infomercials in my time, but this was by far one of my ALL time favorites – Why? I got to include my whole family for the very first time.  The product was a frozen food home delivery company (they shall remain nameless as the show tested well and they decided NOT to roll it out because (as I was told) they did n’t want to grow the business that fast — go figure!

The pictures say it all and I’m sorry the world didn’t get to enjoy this show – it was a true sit-com!
