TV Host Tia Castle

From the original Forbes Factor · August 12, 2017

Tia is an exceptional video journalist who is a self proclaimed “Jill of all trades and a master of some.”  Not only is she the host of a luxury real estate and lifestyle television show “Home and Castle TV” but she is also the creator and producer.  “My background in broadcast news, film and corporate sales is a combination that contributes to a unique style within my productions.”

Ms. Castle is a talented writer having produced many commercial spots for the luxury industry as well as appeared as talent and or host within the spots. Her expertise spans several industries from real estate to medical to beauty and fashion. Home and Castle TV can be viewed in several cities in the US and Canada.
