Lola Jeans Make you look SKINNY!

By Forbes Riley · November 24, 2015

Magic – I put the jeans on and wow – I look like I lost 10 pounds!  Also at my age, I would never consider wearing “skinny” tight legged jeans — well I have found my dream pair.  We had so much fun with these 4 ways stretched jeans that I didn’t want to take them off and went to the website and bought 4 more in different cuts and of course added black!

Also a shout out to the energetic and passionate Kasie Savage who made laugh out loud I almost split my seams!

Brothers David and Sam were never straight A students and therefore never got an allowance from their parents. They were left with no choice but to quit school at a young age and work at their father’s jeans store. Reluctant at first, they worked by their dad’s side and eventually fell in love with the fashion industry. Searching for a new challenge and with barely any experience in manufacturing and wholesaling, they started designing and producing jeans. Their goal: Produce the most comfortable yet fashionable women’s jeans.

After years of trial and error, extensive investment, countless sleepless nights, Lola Jeans was finally born in 2005. We believe that all women are beautiful and inspiring, no matter her shape or size. Lola Jeans embodies these beliefs and strives to empower women thru fashion. Lola Jeans’ innovative 4-way stretch denim and curve-hugging jeans assure a flawless fit and unmatched comfort! “Embrace your curves, beauty’s not defined by the size of your jeans.” -LJ
