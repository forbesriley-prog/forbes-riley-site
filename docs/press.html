Medium · 110 words

Forbes Riley is recognized internationally as an award-winning TV host, spokesperson, celebrity fitness and lifestyle expert, keynote speaker and author. Host of more than 180 national infomercials with over $2.5 billion in sales across 80 countries, she launched Fit-TV with Jake Steinfeld (sold to FOX for $500 million), hosted two national talk shows and ESPN’s X-Games, and created SpinGym, which sold 61,000 units in 24 hours on live home shopping and has passed a million sold. Inducted into the National Fitness Hall of Fame in 2010, Forbes now teaches the art of the pitch through Forbes Factor Live, FRedX and Momentum X. She is the mother of twins.

Fast facts

More about Forbes

  • Media pioneer who has hosted more than 180 infomercials, reaching over $2.5 billion in sales in more than 80 countries.
  • Inducted into the US National Fitness Hall of Fame (2010); a 25-year career promoting health and fitness on television worldwide.
  • Launched the TV series Fit-TV with Jake “Body by Jake” Steinfeld; the network sold to FOX for $500 million.
  • Creator and host of Forbes Living on WEtv, hosting entrepreneurs and launching new business ideas.
  • Host of Essentials, a daily national talk show on TLC, and the premiere years of ESPN’s X-Games.
  • Created SpinGym: 61,000 units sold in 24 hours on live home shopping; over one million sold.
  • Multiple ERA Moxie Awards for Best Female Presenter, two Telly Awards, Miss Teenage New York.
  • Broadway credits opposite Christopher Reeve; TV credits on 24, The Practice, Picket Fences, Fashion House.
  • Certified personal trainer, licensed massage therapist, NLP master, brown belt in Tae Kwon Do.
  • Mother of boy and girl twins. Homes in Los Angeles and St. Petersburg, Florida.
