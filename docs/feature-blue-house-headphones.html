Blue House Headphones

From the original Forbes Factor · August 12, 2017

Meet Tim Noe, Founder and CEO of Blue House Records LLC – http://www.thisisblue.house/ which was founded with the mission of creating and distributing the best music of our generation. To do that, we made mp3 headphones to store our music. Now they run with bluetooth.

Blue House Records LLC is a record label that distributes music on SC cards…which you can plug right into its MP3 Headphones. Blue House aims to bring tangibility back to music, and make some phenomenal aural sensations along the way.

You can upload your music directly onto them via the included SD card. AKA no wires, no external device, and all bliss.

Turn on the headphone, then plug into your computer with the USB cable. Drag and drop your audio files onto the disk titled “BlueHouse”. Use .mp3 format!
