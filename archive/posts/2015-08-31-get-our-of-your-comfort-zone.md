# Get our of your comfort zone

Date: 2015-08-31
Original URL: https://forbesfactor.com/daily-inspiration/get-our-of-your-comfort-zone/
Categories: DAILY INSPIRATION

---

Once you get rid of the idea that you must please other people in order to feel accepted before you please yourself, and begin to follow your own instincts, only then can you be successful. You’ll feel more fulfilled and at peace within and other people tend to be satisfied by what you do. Understand that at times you have to leave the city of your own comfort and go into the wilderness of your intuition.   Follow your gut, because when you do, what you’ll discover will be amazing – what you’ll discover is actually yourself. Remember, there are no mistakes, only lessons. Love yourself, trust your choices, and everything is possible.
