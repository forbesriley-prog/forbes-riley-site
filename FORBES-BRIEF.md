Paste this whole file into a new chat before asking for the workbook. It is the full picture from the website rebuild, the memory wall and the Instagram series.

## Who she is (one line)
Dr. Forbes Riley, "The Queen of Pitch". Award-winning TV host, the most successful female infomercial presenter of all time ($2.5 billion in sales, 80 countries, 180+ national infomercials), creator of SpinGym, keynote speaker, author and coach. Signature line: "You are the sum of the obstacles you overcome."

## Her story, in the order she tells it
- Teens: Miss Teenage New York (did not win Miss Teenage America). Her father spent three years in hospital after an accident, 15 operations, through her whole high school. He wanted to be an inventor and never got to build it. She built everything "for both of us". She still starts every pitch with his name in her head.
- 20s: Broadway musicals, played opposite Christopher Reeve, worked with Bob Fosse. Backpacked solo through Europe at 21. Eight shows a week while carrying an extra 30 pounds and hiding it. "Great on stage, miserable in the mirror."
- 1990s: ESPN's original X-Games, TLC's daily talk show Essentials, Fit-TV with Body by Jake (network later sold to FOX for $500M), 20+ years on HSN and QVC (US, Canada, England, Spain, Australia). Acting: 24, The Practice, Picket Fences, lead in Fashion House.
- 2000s: the "$2 Billion Host". Jack LaLanne Power Juicer (eight years, 80 countries) and Montel Williams HealthMaster. Twins Ryker and Makenna born 2002.
- 2009: SpinGym launched on Discovery's PitchMen after being told it would never work. Sold out of the back of her car, then 600,000 in three years, 61,000 in one 24-hour live show, over a million today. US rehab patent.
- 2010: National Fitness Hall of Fame (with Arnold Schwarzenegger, Joe Weider, Tony Little). ERA Moxie Awards for Best Female Presenter, two Telly Awards.
- 2015: Forbes Riley Studios, her own production studio and sound stage in St. Petersburg, Florida. Forbes Living on WEtv.
- 2019: Forbes Factor Live (five-day pitch and mindset program). Forbes Factor podcast on VoiceAmerica.
- Today: teaching the skill that built all of it. Lives between Los Angeles and St. Petersburg. Skis, scuba dives, reads constantly ("Read the best books first").

## What she teaches (the product)
Everything funnels into pitching. Core ideas she repeats:
- Your WHY is your first pitch. Say it in one sentence.
- The 7-second hook. Nobody buys the product, they buy the reason.
- The three pitch patterns behind $2.5 billion in sales. How not to sound salesy.
- Posture first, feeling second: feet, shoulders, breath, then speak. "The walk" into every room, even on Zoom.
- Your pitch is a performance. Reps are the only way it becomes second nature. Rehearse out loud ten times, then it's yours.
- Engage, excite, enroll. A YES opens doors.
- Positioning, story, the ask, objections, the close.
- Reinvention: Dream It, Believe It, Achieve It. Drop limiting beliefs.
- "If you don't build your dreams, someone will hire you to build theirs."

## Voice and style
- First person, direct, warm, a little theatrical. Short punchy lines. She names the fear, then the fix.
- Story first, lesson second, action third. Every story ends in something the reader does today.
- Self-deprecating about the hard parts (the weight, the losses) and unapologetic about the wins (the $2.5 billion).
- Likes word triplets and commands: "Feet. Shoulders. Breath." "Engage, excite, enroll."
- Nicknames she accepts: Queen of Pitch, the $2.5 Billion Host, "the female Tony Robbins".
- Brand look: black and gold, pale gold headline text (#E8D9A0), white body, her script logo. TAN Harmoni font for headlines. No pink, no emoji clutter.
- Friends and mentors to quote: Les Brown, Joe Theismann, Florence Henderson, Jack LaLanne, Kurt Angle.
