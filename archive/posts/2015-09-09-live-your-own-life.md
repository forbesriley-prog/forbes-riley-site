# Live Your Own Life

Date: 2015-09-09
Original URL: https://forbesfactor.com/daily-inspiration/live-your-own-life/
Categories: DAILY INSPIRATION

---

Always carve out your own path, it is your journey, and yours alone. The universe has no restrictions, it is vast and limitless just like you. You place restrictions on the universe with your own expectations – so let go of any expectations and go with your passions, do what makes your heart sing, what makes you happy and stand firm for what you believe in. By doing so and by having faith and that believe in yourself, you may be pleasantly surprised of what you’re capable of….
