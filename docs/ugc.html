Before you book

Does she post the video on her own accounts?

Only if you add it. The standard package is content for your channels and ads. Posting to her feed, or whitelisting her accounts for your ads, is an add-on with its own rights.

What products does she say yes to?

Things she would use or give to a friend: fitness, kitchen, beauty, wellness, home, gifts, books and courses. She will tell you quickly if it is not a fit.

Who writes the script?

She does, from your brief and your must-say claims. You approve every word before she shoots.

How are usage rights handled?

Organic use on your own channels is included. Paid ads, whitelisting and anything beyond 12 months are priced separately and written into the quote.

Can she do it in a day?

Rush delivery in 48 hours is an add-on, product in hand. Ask when you send the brief.
