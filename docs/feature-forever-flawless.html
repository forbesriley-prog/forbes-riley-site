Forever Flawless

From the original Forbes Factor · August 12, 2017

Meet my guest Ellie Parisi, Spokesperson for Forever Flawless – http://www.foreverflawless.com/.

Diamonds, the most precious gemstones, have been widely recognized for their exceptional therapeutic powers and miraculous abilities. In ancient times, diamonds were regarded as the gemstones of Venus, the Roman goddess that represents beauty, love, and fertility. In modern times, diamonds are considered as a symbol of purity, exclusivity, and perfection. There is mounting scientific evidence that associates diamonds with health benefits and improved well-being. Diamonds are also increasingly being used for modern skin exfoliation techniques, like microdermabrasion and diamond peeling treatments performed at high-end spas.

Forever Flawless — Diamond Infused Skin Care — is designed to deliver spa quality results in the comfort of one’s home. By utilizing cutting edge technologies, and by developing natural diamond powder infused formulas, Forever Flawless has successfully combined the proven benefits of professional diamond microdermabrasion and peeling treatments to satisfy the desire of many individuals who want to look and feel at their best and defy the aging process.

Forever Flawless offers five diamond infused collections, each to target signs of premature aging. Each product of the line contains micronized, natural, and genuine diamond powder as an exfoliating agent. This unique approach ensures the delivery of the most advanced active and age defying ingredients into the deeper layers of the skin. Within seconds, the Forever Flawless line provides WOW results and the skin looks much more radiant, revitalized, and refreshed. Within a few applications, users note that their wrinkles and fine lines diminish and other skin concerns, such as age spots, acne, and stretch marks, are significantly improved. When used long-term, consumers realize much better looking and healthier skin.
