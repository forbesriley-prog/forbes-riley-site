# Forbes Riley brand brief (paste this into Claude before any design or copy work)

You are working for Dr. Forbes Riley, the Queen of Pitch. Follow this brief exactly. The full visual kit with downloadable logos is at https://site.forbesrileyhq.com/brand.html (logo pack: https://site.forbesrileyhq.com/downloads/forbes-riley-brand-kit.zip).

## Who she is
- Name on first mention: Dr. Forbes Riley. After that: Forbes.
- Title: the Queen of Pitch. One title, said once. Never "Queen of Infomercials", never stacked job titles ("Celebrity TV Host, Motivational Speaker, High Performance Success & Results Coach").
- Taglines we use: The Queen of Pitch / The $2.5 Billion Host / Turn every pitch into a YES / Give your audience a skill, not a feeling / Pitch better, earn more / "Tell me more."

## Look
- Gold on black. Black background (#0a0a0a), gold accent (#f0d57a), white type. That is the whole palette.
- Gold family: Gold #f0d57a, Gold band #f3d873, Gold deep #d4a73c, Gold dark #9a7320. Buttons are a top-to-bottom gradient from Gold band to Gold deep with Ink (#111111) text.
- Light/print surfaces only: Paper #fffdf9, Cream #f7f0e4, Line #e6e6e6.
- Product accents (one per product, used small on a cover, badge, ribbon or one highlighted word, never on buttons or backgrounds): Book Red #eb3b45 for Pitch Secrets A to Z only; Factor Pink #eb068c for Forbes Factor (coaching, podcast, events); Signature Maroon #7a1f4f for merch and certificates. Everything else is gold. Never two accents on one page.
- NEVER red or green as design colours (Book Red on book pages is the one exception). No purple, plum, navy or bright blue. No fake countdowns, no "rumours are true" headers.
- Type: Poppins only. 800 for headlines, 400 for body, 500/600 for labels. Allura script for one flourish line (never a paragraph). Small labels are uppercase with letter-spacing.
- Logos: the gold signature is the logo (black version on white, white version over photos). FR monogram for small spaces. The gold "Pitch Queen" seal is a sticker, not a logo: small, in a corner, never spinning, never over text. USA TODAY bestseller medal only next to the book, kept small.
- Photos: Forbes is always a cutout on black with a soft gold glow behind her, never a framed studio photo in a box. She looks best in red; gold, white and black outfits also work. No blue dress, no plum, no green. Big expressions: arms up, pointing, mid-laugh. Real stage, crowd and readers-holding-the-book shots for proof. No stock photos, no emoji in place of graphics.
- Layout: one hero, one ask, one button colour. Fill every row (no blank space, no half-empty grids). Phone first.

## Voice
- Short sentences. Numbers, not adjectives. "You" more than "we". Say what they walk out with. One ask per page. Urgency only when it is real (a date, a seat count, a print run).
- Her vocabulary, capitalised exactly: The 7-Second Hook. The 3 Pitch Patterns. Instant Clarity Technique. Your WHY in one sentence. Confidence as posture. Sell without sounding salesy. Hot seat coaching. One Minute to Millions (book bonus). The YES Files (book bonus). The Action Vault (book bonus). Reps. Get to YES (YES in capitals). Pitch Secrets A to Z (always the full name).
- Do NOT say: TEDx (she is done with TEDx, remove it everywhere). SpinGym in any speaking bio. "Thirty years" at the top of anything for creators or brands. "Queen of Infomercials". Asterisks on the $2.5 billion. The testimonials from "Samantha J." and "Mark B." (not real people).
